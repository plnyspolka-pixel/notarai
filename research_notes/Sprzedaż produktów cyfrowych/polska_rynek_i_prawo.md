## 1. Market size and growth: online courses, e-books/audiobooks, e-learning, subscriptions, share of Poles buying digital content

### Takeaway
Poland is a mature online-shopping market (about 70-78% of internet users buy online), but no public source isolates the creator-sold course/e-book segment; the only "market size" figures are commercial estimates of the whole e-learning market (roughly EUR 0.3 bn to USD 1.5 bn depending on scope). Paid digital reading is still a minority habit and the leading subscription seller (Legimi) shrank in 2025.

### Cited Findings
- GUS: 69.7% of people aged 16-74 bought online in the previous 12 months in 2025 (+2.3 pp y/y); the 2026 edition reports 72.9%. 96.2% of households had internet access in 2025. — [GUS 2025](https://stat.gov.pl/obszary-tematyczne/nauka-i-technika-spoleczenstwo-informacyjne/spoleczenstwo-informacyjne/spoleczenstwo-informacyjne-w-polsce-w-2025-r-,2,15.html); [GUS 2026 press note](https://stat.gov.pl/dla-mediow/informacje-prasowe/ai-zakupy-i-sprawy-urzedowe-tak-polacy-korzystaja-z-internetu-w-2026-roku,57,1.html)
- Gemius "E-commerce w Polsce 2024": 78% of Polish internet users had bought online at least once; the 2025 edition gives nearly 78% (about 30 million people). — [iFirma summary of Gemius 2024](https://www.ifirma.pl/blog/czy-polacy-coraz-czesciej-robia-zakupy-za-granica-omowienie-raportu-gemius-e-commerce-w-polsce-2024); [Przelewy24 on Gemius 2025](https://www.przelewy24.pl/en/news/ecommerce-2025-online-shopping-trends-polish-consumers)

- Gemius "E-commerce w Polsce 2026" exists (Gemius, PBI, IAB Polska, Akademia Leona Koźmińskiego); coverage stresses price, delivery convenience, security and AI-assisted shopping. Category shares for digital content were not retrievable. — [ITwiz](https://itwiz.pl/?p=75765)
- Whole Polish e-commerce: estimates for 2025 range from about PLN 92 bn (KIG, +6.8% y/y) to PLN 140-150+ bn (other sources); PwC/Strategy& forecast PLN 192 bn in 2028 (forecast made in 2024). E-commerce was 9.6% of retail sales in March 2026 (GUS). — [KIG Miesięcznik e-handlu](https://kig.pl/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Miesiecznik-e-handlu_2026-04-24.pdf); [Rzeczpospolita on PwC](https://pro.rp.pl/raporty-ekonomiczne/art41587121-prognozy-gospodarcze-polacy-wydadza-miliardy-na-zakupy-internetowe)
- E-learning market estimates: Ken Research puts the Polish "e-learning and digital education" market at USD 1,500 m in 2025 (USD 880 m in 2020; projected USD 3,275 m by 2032, 11.8% CAGR). Statista projected online-education revenue of EUR 311.7 m and online learning platforms of USD 236.6 m for 2024. Different scopes; not additive. — [Ken Research](https://www.kenresearch.com/poland-e-learning-and-digital-education-market.md); [Statista online education](https://www.statista.com/outlook/emo/online-education/poland); [Statista platforms](https://statista.com/outlook/emo/online-education/online-learning-platforms/poland)
- EU benchmark (Eurostat, 2025): 78% of EU internet users bought online; 9.5% bought e-books or audiobooks (7.3% a year earlier); 12% downloaded games. 34.8% of EU internet users took an online course or used online learning material in 2025; 17.3% took an online course (participation, not purchase). — [Brussels Times on Eurostat](https://www.brusselstimes.com/2093676/digital-reading-in-eu-surges-as-nearly-1-in-10-buy-e-books); [ecommercegermany on Eurostat](https://ecommercegermany.com/blog/top-e-commerce-statistics-for-individuals-eurostat-statistics-explained/); [Eurostat online learning 2025](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/products-eurostat-news/w/edn-20260319-1)

- Biblioteka Narodowa: 41% of adult Poles read at least one book in 2025 (stable); people buy fewer books and borrow more from libraries. - Legimi group: consolidated revenue PLN 108.3 m in 2025 versus PLN 120.2 m in 2024 (about -10%), attributed to changes in the subscriber mix after the Q4 2024 offer change and to a hacking incident that took printed books off the platform in August-September 2025; B2B is over 20% of revenue; market value PLN 61.5 m at end of 2025. — [Legimi annual report 2025](https://newconnect.pl/ebi/files/gpwdata/1750_20260323_213900_20260323_Legimi-Skonsolidowany-Raport-Roczny-2025.pdf); [BiznesRadar](https://www.biznesradar.pl/a/134115,legimi-notuje-rekordowe-wyniki-pomimo-trudnej-koncowki-roku)

- PARP study of the development-services sector: 75% of entities use or plan to use remote educational services. — [PARP report 2025](https://redakcja.parp.gov.pl/storage/publications/pdf/Rozwoj-innowacyjnosc-i-zarzadzanie-w-sektorze-UR-26_03_2025.pdf)

### Inferences
- A consumer subscription for reading content anchors at PLN 30-50 per month; a paid newsletter or membership priced far above that needs a professional (B2B) value argument.
- Because Legimi's B2B share is above 20% and consumer revenue fell, the B2B channel looks more resilient than B2C for subscription content.

## 2. Polish creator / info-product ecosystem: published revenues, price points, niches

### Takeaway
Publicly reported Polish cases show that single launches reach PLN 1-3 m when an audience already exists, the best-selling courses cost at most about PLN 500, and most new creators earn little; one well-documented seller reports a roughly 70% revenue fall for short technical courses since generative AI.

### Cited Findings
- Michał Szafrański, "Finansowy Ninja" (self-published book, 2016): pre-sale passed PLN 1 m revenue with over 11 thousand copies; press reported PLN 1.4 m in 11 months and over 110 thousand copies sold by May 2018. Figures are self-reported and relayed by press. — [Antyweb](https://antyweb.pl/michal-szafranski-ksiazka-sprzedaz); [Antyweb, "milion dolarów"](https://antyweb.pl/milion-dolarow-przychodu-z-jednej-ksiazki-w-polsce-szafranski-bije-rekordy); [Super Biznes](https://superbiz.se.pl/wiadomosci/finansowy-ninja-zarobil-juz-setki-tysiecy-zlotych-aa-yLxz-kBau-vDZG.html)
- Pani Swojego Czasu (Ola Budzyńska), per Forbes Women interview: at its peak the firm employed 64 people and sold PLN 16 m a year; the membership "Klub Pań Swojego Czasu", sold twice a year (May, November), brought on average about PLN 3 m per sales month; a physical venue cost PLN 1.4 m; the business later collapsed ("upadek imperium", 2026 interview). — [Forbes Women](https://www.forbes.pl/forbeswomen/upadek-imperium-pani-swojego-czasu-ola-budzynska-desperacko-skalowalam-firme/49nsyde)
- Maciej Aniserowicz: the course "Droga Nowoczesnego Architekta" took PLN 2 m in eight days of pre-sale before any lesson was recorded (2019). — [Spider's Web](https://spidersweb.pl/2019/06/maciej-aniserowicz-dna-2-mln.html)
- Jakub Mrugalski (own thread): annual gross revenue of his short "in an hour" Linux courses was PLN 698k (2022), 643k (2023), 264k (2024), 185k (2025, marked as a simulation); unit prices about PLN 47-115, mostly under 100; he attributes the decline largely to generative AI. — [Typefully thread](https://typefully.com/uwteam/zarobki-z-kursow-online-vs-ai-DIB1utY)
- myVOD platform: its top five creators made PLN 8.5 m in 2021 from over 46 thousand course units (an implied average of about PLN 185 per unit — my arithmetic); programming courses about PLN 270k a year, sport about PLN 230k; top sellers favour bundles. — [Antyweb](https://antyweb.pl/rekordzisci-sprzedaja-niemal-20-tysiecy-sztuk-kursow-kursy-online-droga-do-monetyzacji-wiedzy)
- Forbes: the price of the most frequently sold courses in Poland does not exceed PLN 500; boom in courses teaching manual skills; report "Ekonomia Wiedzy w Polsce. Jak Polacy zarabiają na wiedzy eksperckiej i produktach cyfrowych" (Fundacja BeWOW): over half of surveyed digital creators are aged 35-44; 89% of creators active under a year do not reach PLN 100k annual revenue; 62% see large growth potential, 8% see saturation (the last three figures came through a search summary — to verify in the article). — [Forbes](https://www.forbes.pl/biznes/nauka-kraula-i-obslugi-koparki-trwa-boom-na-kursy-online-manualnych-umiejetnosci/lslqcld)
- "Ekonomia twórców po polsku 2024": brand deals are 44.7% of creators' income; 31.8% offer courses, training or consultations; 13.4% sell digital content such as e-books or audiobooks; one in three creators selling own products earns over PLN 10k a month. — [Antyweb](https://antyweb.pl/zarobki-influencerow-w-polsce-2024); [iFirma](https://www.ifirma.pl/blog/jak-zarabia-tworca-internetowy-influencer-w-polsce/)
- ClickMeeting "State of Online Events 2025": average paid webinar ticket PLN 99. — [ClickMeeting](https://knowledge.clickmeeting.com/uploads/2026/01/stateofonlineevents-2025.pdf)
- AI_devs (Mrugalski, Gospodarczyk, Chrobok) reached a fourth edition; no participant or revenue figure found. — [LinkedIn post](https://pl.linkedin.com/in/emilczyrko)

### Inferences
- Observed price ladder: PLN 47-115 short course, about PLN 99 webinar, up to PLN 500 mainstream course, cohort or membership launches for existing audiences above that.
- Every large case relied on an owned audience built over years (blog, newsletter); these numbers say nothing about cold-start tests.

### Gaps
- No self-published or reputably reported figures found for Kamila Rowińska or Mirosław Burnejko; none for AI_devs.
- Niche popularity ranking (finance, IT, AI, languages, law/tax, health, parenting) has no quantitative Polish source in what I found.

## 3. Polish sales platforms, payment methods and marketplaces

### Takeaway
BLIK is the default payment method and must be in any checkout; course platforms publish almost no sales data. KFS-funded training must since 2026 be delivered by a provider registered in the Baza Usług Rozwojowych (see section 4).

### Cited Findings
- BLIK Q3 2026: 822 m transactions worth PLN 131.8 bn (+10% count, +17% value y/y), average transaction PLN 160. Q1 2026: 756 m transactions, PLN 117.3 bn. H1 2026: e-commerce was 48.5% of all BLIK transactions. Q3 2025: 348.7 m online transactions worth PLN 54.2 bn out of 743.9 m. — [bank.pl Q3 2026](https://bank.pl/?p=564233); [BLIK H1 2026](https://www.blik.com/en/blik-delivers-a-strong-first-half-of-2026-1-6-billion-transactions-and-over-2-million-more-active-users-year-on-year); [BLIK Q3 2025](https://www.blik.com/en/over-740-m-blik-transactions-in-the-third-quarter-of-2025)
- Autopay 2026: 65% of online buyers use BLIK and 49% call it their favourite method; 59% dislike deferred payments and their fees. — [bank.pl on Autopay](https://bank.pl/autopay-o-polskim-rynku-platnosci-online-na-tle-rozwiazan-europejskich/)
- Gemius 2025 as cited by Shoper: pay-by-link second (64%), card third, cash on delivery 33%; a 78% BLIK figure is attributed to Gemius inconsistently — treat with caution. — [Shoper](https://www.shoper.pl/learn/artykul/5-lat-shoper-platnosci-jak-zmienialy-sie-nawyki-kupujacych-online)
- Tpay "Jak Polacy lubią płacić online 2026" (5th edition): BLIK still dominates; headline says almost half of e-consumers abandon purchases for one simple reason (the reason was not visible). — [Tpay press note](https://tpay.com/user/assets/files_for_download/news/nowy-raport-e-commerce-tpay-notka%20prasowa.pdf); [Tpay report](https://tpay.com/user/assets/files_for_download/jak-polacy-lubia-placic-online-2026.pdf)
- EasyCart/Easytools self-reported counters: 100 m (currency not stated) earned for creators, 324,987 transactions, 32,112 products. — [easy.tools](https://easy.tools)
- Publigo integrates with Shoper (Shoper takes payment and invoices, Publigo creates the learner account). — [Shoper app store](https://www.shoper.pl/aplikacje/publigo)
- Academic: BLIK and fast transfers are the most popular methods (RO019); BNPL users are mainly young, educated women using it to try before paying (RO021); BNPL adoption is driven by usefulness, risk and innovativeness, not ease of use (RO022). — [OpenAlex W4417116914](https://openalex.org/W4417116914); [W7140495242](https://openalex.org/W7140495242); [W7123908085](https://openalex.org/W7123908085)

### Inferences
- With an average BLIK ticket of PLN 160 and e-commerce at about half of BLIK volume, a product under roughly PLN 200 fits a one-code impulse purchase; dearer products justify instalments or deferred payment.

### Gaps
- No published sales data found for Publigo, WebToLearn, Naffy, Sellizer/Selly, MailingR, Udemy PL, Eduj, Strefa Kursów or Allegro digital goods. No measured effect of BLIK on conversion (only preference shares). PayPo/Twisto volumes not found.

## 4. Consumer behaviour relevant to conversion; B2B purchasing and public co-financing (KFS, BUR)

### Takeaway
Trust in the seller's site and prior good experience drive Polish purchase intention; the Polish online buyer is now older and price-comparing. For B2B, registration in BUR became the gateway to KFS money in 2026, which makes BUR a real sales channel for courses.

### Cited Findings
- KFS from 1 January 2026 runs under the Act of 20 March 2025 on the labour market and employment services (Dz.U. 2025 poz. 620) and an MRPiPS regulation of 25 November 2025: applications are electronic via the employer's account at the powiat labour office; training financed from KFS may be delivered only by a provider entered in the Baza Usług Rozwojowych; per-applicant annual limits were introduced; the applicant must have paid Labour Fund contributions for at least 6 months (or be exempt). — [PUP Rzeszów rules 2026](https://rzeszow.praca.gov.pl/documents/d/powiatowy-urzad-pracy-w-rzeszowie/zasady-kfs-2026); [PUP Miechów rules](https://miechow.praca.gov.pl/documents/d/powiatowy-urzad-pracy-w-miechowie/zasady-finansowania-ksztalcenia-ustawicznego-z-kfs-w-2026-r-); [PUP Ostrowiec](https://ostrowiec.praca.gov.pl/documents/d/powiatowy-urzad-pracy-w-ostrowcu-swietokrzyskim/zmiany-w-ofercie-krajowego-funduszu-szkoleniowego)
- BUR/KFS programmes are described as covering up to 90% or 100% of training cost (general press claim). BUR is financed from FERS; a new "Standard usługi rozwojowej" and revised BUR rules apply from 5 May 2026; many BUR listings are remote real-time services; PARP publishes a market price study of BUR services. — [Infor](https://ksiegowosc.infor.pl/obrot-gospodarczy/finanse-i-inwestycje/7554102,dofinansowanie-szkolen-pracownikow-w-2026-r-panstwo-wylozy-do-90-proc-kosztow-rozwoju-kompetencji.html); [PARP, standard from 5 May 2026](https://www.parp.gov.pl/component/content/article/90425%3Astandard-uslugi-rozwojowej-od-5-maja-2026-r-najwazniejsze-zmiany); [PARP price study](https://www.parp.gov.pl/component/publications/publication/badanie-cen-rynkowych-uslug-rozwojowych-bur)
- Trust in websites and positive past experience directly raise purchase intention of young Polish consumers (RO011). — [OpenAlex W4312847758](https://openalex.org/W4312847758)
- Between 2016 and 2021 the Polish e-consumer became more often university-educated and over 35, and increasingly compares prices online (RO013); survey waves 2012 (n=972) and 2023 (n=1161) show higher frequency, spend and product range (RO012). — [W4396891737](https://openalex.org/W4396891737); [W7225448248](https://openalex.org/W7225448248)
- UOKiK treats checkbox-only "I bought it" review verification as insufficient (charges against Samsung Electronics Polska, January 2026). — [Bankier](https://www.bankier.pl/wiadomosc/Zarzuty-UOKiK-dla-Samsung-Electronics-Polska-Spolka-moze-wprowadzac-w-blad-klientow-9068003.html)

### Inferences
- A course that is "BUR-ready" (real-time remote delivery, registered provider, standard-compliant card) can be bought by firms with public money; purely self-paced recordings may not qualify — check the 5 May 2026 standard.
- The fake "EU subsidy" case in section 7 shows the regulator watches subsidy claims closely; advertise KFS/BUR financing only when the service is actually registered.

### Gaps
- KFS 2026 national budget and exact co-financing rates (commonly 80% / 100% for micro-firms — to verify) not confirmed. Gemius, Trusted Shops and Opineo figures on reviews and trust factors not retrieved. No Polish data on attitudes to subscriptions.

## 5. Polish-language and Poland-focused academic research (OpenAlex)

### Takeaway
Poland-specific research is descriptive (surveys of e-consumers, BLIK, BNPL, influencers); causal evidence on tactics comes from international experiments and meta-analyses, and it weakens the case for urgency tricks while supporting honest disclosure.

### Cited Findings
- Showing the lowest prior 30-day price significantly reduced purchase intention in a pre-registered experiment (n=500), more for utilitarian products. — [W7117315611](https://openalex.org/W7117315611)
- Deceptive countdown timers added little beyond the discount itself and were judged manipulative (N=245); scarcity cues reduced perceived vendor benevolence and user experience (N=202). — [W4366586156](https://openalex.org/W4366586156); [W4387021653](https://openalex.org/W4387021653)
- Meta-analysis of 416 effect sizes from 131 studies: time-based scarcity works best for high-involvement products, demand-based for utilitarian ones; a second meta-analysis finds demand scarcity effective but not urgency scarcity. — [W4284967436](https://openalex.org/W4284967436); [W4367182778](https://openalex.org/W4367182778)
- Mild dark patterns more than doubled sign-ups and aggressive ones almost quadrupled them; hidden information, trick questions and obstruction were most effective; "must act now" messages did not raise purchases. — [W3137759237](https://openalex.org/W3137759237)
- 1,818 dark-pattern instances on about 11K shopping sites; 22 third parties sell them as turnkey plug-ins. — [W4288086176](https://openalex.org/W4288086176)
- Displayed advertising status increased influencer credibility (364 respondents). — [W4283361299](https://openalex.org/W4283361299)
- Polish doctrine on the 2023 implementation of Directives 2019/770 and 2019/771: new conformity criteria, qualified consent for deviations, updates, partial withdrawal. — [W4383720634](https://openalex.org/W4383720634)

### Inferences
- The tactics that "work" most in experiments are exactly the ones Polish and EU law prohibit; genuine demand proof and genuine deadlines are both lawful and supported by evidence.

### Gaps
- OpenAlex returned no Polish study on buying online courses, e-books or paid newsletters, none on lead magnets or email consent, and none measuring conversion effects in Poland. Polish-language keyword queries ("kursy online e-learning Polska", "treści cyfrowe konsument prawo odstąpienia") returned zero hits; only "treści cyfrowe" returned legal-definition papers.

## 6. Consumer Rights Act after the 2023 implementation (digital content, withdrawal, conformity)

### Takeaway
A consumer may withdraw from a distance contract within 14 days unless the seller of digital content collects express prior consent, an acknowledgement of losing the right, and sends a confirmation; a regulations clause alone does not remove the right. Mixed courses (recordings plus live sessions) must be assessed element by element.

### Cited Findings
- The Act of 30 May 2014 on consumer rights has a consolidated text Dz.U. 2026 poz. 1244 (earlier Dz.U. 2024 poz. 1796). — [Sejm ELI](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2026/1244/text/O/D20261244.pdf)
- Art. 38 ust. 1 pkt 13: no withdrawal right for digital content not supplied on a tangible medium for which the consumer must pay, if performance began with the consumer's express prior consent, the consumer was informed before performance that they will lose the right and acknowledged it, and the trader provided the confirmation referred to in art. 15 ust. 1 and 2 or art. 21 ust. 1. All conditions are cumulative. — [iFirma commentary](https://www.ifirma.pl/blog/zwrot-produktow-cyfrowych-w-sklepie-internetowym-czy-mozna-zwrocic-kurs-online-lub-e-book/)
- A statement in the terms that "digital products are non-returnable" is not enough; for courses combining recordings with consultations or live classes each performance must be classified, since digital content and services follow different rules. — [iFirma commentary](https://www.ifirma.pl/blog/zwrot-produktow-cyfrowych-w-sklepie-internetowym-czy-mozna-zwrocic-kurs-online-lub-e-book/)
- Art. 2 pkt 5 defines digital content as data produced and supplied in digital form. Chapter 5b (art. 43h-43q) implements Directive 2019/770: art. 43k sets conformity criteria and the duty to inform about and supply updates (including security updates); for continuous supply the content must stay conforming for the contract period; art. 43l sets the trader's liability. In force from 1 January 2023. — [LexLege chapter 5b](https://lexlege.pl/ustawa-o-prawach-konsumenta/rozdzial-5b-umowy-o-dostarczanie-tresci-cyfrowej-lub-uslugi-cyfrowej/15770/); [LexLege art. 43k](https://lexlege.pl/ustawa-o-prawach-konsumenta/art-43k/); [UOKiK guide "Rewolucja konsumencka 2023"](https://prawakonsumenta.uokik.gov.pl/pliki/rewolucja-konsumencka-2023-przewodnik-po-zmianach-w-prawie-konsumenckim.pdf)
- The rules also cover contracts where the consumer "pays" with personal data instead of money; they do not apply when data are collected only to supply the content. — [Poradnik Przedsiębiorcy](https://poradnikprzedsiebiorcy.pl/-umowy-oplacone-danymi-osobowymi-a-konsekwencje-ich-rozwiazania); [PARP](https://www.parp.gov.pl/component/content/article/83623:dyrektywa-omnibus-platnosc-danymi-osobowymi)
- UOKiK has challenged language-course sellers for not telling consumers before performance that they would lose the withdrawal right. — [UOKiK document](https://uokik.gov.pl/Download/1925)

### Inferences
- Checkout for an e-book or recorded course needs an unticked consent box with the loss-of-right wording plus a confirmation email on a durable medium; without it the 14-day right stays.

### Gaps (to verify in the statute text)
- "Przedsiębiorca na prawach konsumenta": my search found no source; from memory the rule is art. 7aa of the Act and art. 385(5) of the Civil Code, in force since 1 January 2021, giving sole traders consumer protection (withdrawal, unfair terms) when the contract is not of a professional character for them, judged in particular by CEIDG activity codes — to verify.
- Liability period (two years) and one-year presumption for digital content, and the 14-day refund deadline, were not confirmed in sources (art. 43l-43n to verify).
- Pre-ticked boxes for extra payments (art. 10) and the "zamówienie z obowiązkiem zapłaty" button (art. 17): from memory, not confirmed in sources — to verify.

## 7. Omnibus price rules and UOKiK enforcement

### Takeaway
Every announced discount must show the lowest price from the 30 days before the reduction, computed and worded exactly; UOKiK and the Trade Inspection enforce this heavily, with fines of nearly PLN 37 m on Zalando and Temu in January 2026.

### Cited Findings
- Art. 4 ust. 2 of the Act on informing about prices of goods and services requires showing the lowest price in the 30 days before the reduction; art. 6 ust. 1 makes breach of art. 4 ust. 1-5 subject to an administrative fine (Trade Inspection). — [UOKiK DIH decision](https://decyzje.uokik.gov.pl/bp/dec_prez.nsf/43104c28a7a1be23c1257eac006d8dd4/6fcd7e81f762d1a7c1258beb00176817/$FILE/DECYZJA%20DIH-3.99.2024%20uchylenie%20decyzji%20WIIH%20i%20orzeczenie%20co%20do%20istoty_ceny.pdf)
- Decisions from 2023-2024 penalised: the label "najniższa cena z ostatnich 30 dni" because it does not say the period is before the reduction; and a "-70%" calculated from the regular price instead of the 30-day lowest price. — [UOKiK DIH-3.110.2024](https://decyzje.uokik.gov.pl/bp/dec_prez.nsf/43104c28a7a1be23c1257eac006d8dd4/c63dfe4425b174f0c1258beb0017d8c6/$FILE/DECYZJA%20DIH-3.110.2024%20uchylenie%20decyzji%20WIIH%20i%20orzeczenie%20co%20do%20istoty_ceny.pdf); [UOKiK DIH-3.84.2024](https://decyzje.uokik.gov.pl/bp/dec_prez.nsf/43104c28a7a1be23c1257eac006d8dd4/07c00faafd53cb57c1258beb0016c1e4/$FILE/DECYZJA%20DIH-3.84.2024%20uchylenie%20decyzji%20WIIH%20i%20orzeczenie%20co%20do%20istoty_ceny.pdf)
- 12 January 2026: two UOKiK decisions fined Zalando and Temu nearly PLN 37 m in total for improper promotion information including the 30-day lowest price (Temu decision DOZIK-9/2025). Charges were also brought against Black Red White, Żabka Nano, Peek & Cloppenburg, H&M and AzaGroup. — [Spider's Web](https://spidersweb.pl/2026/01/uokik-kary-dla-zalando-i-temu-za-falszywe-promocje.html); [Prawo.pl](https://www.prawo.pl/biznes/zarzuty-uokik-za-nieprawidlowe-oznaczanie-promocji,522125.html); [Prawo.pl H&M](https://www.prawo.pl/biznes/uokik-zarzuty-dla-h-m-i-peek-cloppenburg,1545570.html)
- UOKiK position: never-ending promotions mislead consumers. — [Osborne Clarke](https://www.osborneclarke.com/pl/insights/nieustannie-trwajace-promocje-wprowadzaja-konsumentow-w-blad-poinformowal-prezes-uokik)
- UOKiK in 2025: 900 decisions and PLN 1.15 bn in fines (competition and consumer protection together). — [Spider's Web, April 2026](https://spidersweb.pl/2026/04/uokik-nieuczciwe-opinie-kary.html)
- Experiment: displaying the Omnibus price lowers purchase intention (RO001). — [W7117315611](https://openalex.org/W7117315611)

### Inferences
- A "launch discount" on a product never sold before has no prior price, so a struck-through "regular price" is risky; the safer wording is an introductory price with a stated end date, not a percentage off (my reading — to verify against UOKiK's explanations).
- Recurring launches at the same promo price make the 30-day lowest price equal to the promo price once sales reopen within 30 days.

### Gaps
- Fine ceilings (commonly cited PLN 20,000, and PLN 40,000 for three breaches in 12 months) and UOKiK's treatment of discount codes and introductory prices were not confirmed by a source — to verify.

## 9. Money-back guarantees, income claims, financial-education products, health claims

### Takeaway
Income promises and investment "education" are the highest-risk content: UOKiK treats earnings-for-recruitment schemes as pyramids, and KNF/ESMA say courses can amount to investment advice requiring a licence regardless of disclaimers.

- UOKiK decision RGD-1/2026 concerns Eaconomy LLC (platform eaconomy.io, promoters from "1Vision Polska"), following proceedings opened 2 September 2024 (RGD-97/2024); snippets mention an activation fee for an affiliate kit and partner membership. — [UOKiK decision PDF](https://decyzje.uokik.gov.pl/bp/dec_prez.nsf/43104c28a7a1be23c1257eac006d8dd4/ffa308ddec93f80ec1258dee002f4842/$FILE/decyzja%20Eaconomy%20-%20RGD-1_2026%20wersja%20jawna.pdf)

## 10. Email marketing consent, lead magnets, GDPR, cookies

### Takeaway
Since 10 November 2024 art. 398 of the Electronic Communications Law requires prior consent for commercial email; a freebie may be exchanged for an email address, but marketing emails need a clearly stated, separate and voluntary agreement, and even contacting people to ask for consent is prohibited.

### Cited Findings
- Art. 398 PKE prohibits sending commercial information, including direct marketing, via automated calling systems or telecommunications terminal equipment unless the user consented; consent must be prior, explicit, separate, channel-specific and provable; the UKE President imposes fines. — [Prawo.pl](https://www.prawo.pl/biznes/zgody-marketingowe-i-kontakty-handlowe-co-mowi-prawo-komunikacji-elektronicznej,530981.html); [iFirma](https://www.ifirma.pl/blog/zgoda-marketingowa-jak-prawidlowo-uzyskac-zgode-na-wysylanie-ofert/); [Poradnik Przedsiębiorcy](https://poradnikprzedsiebiorcy.pl/-prawo-komunikacji-elektronicznej-i-zgoda-marketingowa)
- Contact made only to obtain consent is not allowed, as confirmed by UKE and UOKiK decisions (parliamentary interpellation reply; the Ministry of Digital Affairs asked UKE and UODO for views in September 2025). — [Sejm interpellation reply](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/sejm/term10/interpellations/attachment/ATTDMGHNP/i12337-o1.pdf)
- UODO's complaints department was running about 380 marketing cases, mostly about email and post. — [Sejm interpellation reply](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/sejm/term10/interpellations/attachment/ATTDMGHNG/i12182-o1.pdf)
- Lead magnets are permitted under GDPR, but marketing may not be sent to addresses given for a lead magnet unless the form clearly states that subscribers will also receive promotional messages. — [MailerLite](https://www.mailerlite.com/pl/blog/how-to-create-opt-in-forms-that-still-work-under-gdpr)
- Consent must be freely given, specific, informed and unambiguous (GDPR art. 4(11)); EDPB Opinion 08/2024 on "consent or pay" requires a real choice; withdrawal must be as easy as giving consent (art. 7(3)). — [EDPB Opinion 08/2024](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/system/files/2024-11/edpb_opinion_202408_consentorpay_pl.pdf); [PARP](https://www.parp.gov.pl/component/content/article/75021:zgoda-jako-podstawa-prawna-przetwarzania-danych-osobowych-kiedy-nalezy-ja-stosowac)
- A January 2026 case note discusses whether a reminder about a discount is direct marketing and the consent versus legitimate-interest basis. — [Lege Artis](https://czasopismo.legeartis.org/2026/01/marketing-bezposredni-uzasadniony-interes-zgoda-przesylanie-informacji-handlowych-wyrok/)
- Cookies: art. 399 PKE governs storing information on terminal equipment; consent must meet GDPR standards (art. 400 PKE). — [LexLege art. 399](https://lexlege.pl/komunik-elektr/art-399/); [Grant Thornton](https://grantthornton.pl/publikacja/prawo-komunikacji-elektronicznej-a-ochrona-danych-osobowych/)

### Inferences
- Two lawful designs exist: (a) the product is the newsletter itself and the e-book is a bonus, stated plainly; (b) the e-book is delivered for the address and a separate unticked box covers marketing. Whether making the freebie conditional on marketing consent is valid is unsettled; such a contract also falls under the digital-content rules as "payment with data".

### Gaps
- No UODO or EDPB statement specifically on lead magnets was found; no Polish cookie or Meta-pixel fines for 2025-2026 found.

## 11. VAT, KSeF, cash register, JPK, unregistered activity

### Takeaway
A small test can run as unregistered activity up to PLN 10,813.50 revenue per quarter (2026); B2C invoices stay outside mandatory KSeF; electronically delivered e-books and courses can be exempt from the cash register.

### Cited Findings
- Unregistered activity 2026: limit is now quarterly, 225% of the minimum wage (PLN 4,806) = PLN 10,813.50 of due revenue per quarter; after exceeding it, 7 days to register in CEIDG. — [podatki.gov.pl](https://podatki.gov.pl/poradniki-i-informatory/dzialalnosc-nierejestrowana); [money.pl](https://direct.money.pl/artykuly/porady/dzialalnosc-nierejestrowana-2026-limit-10-813,50-zl,-po-przekroczeniu-7-dni-na-ceidg)
- KSeF: mandatory issuing from 1 February 2026 for firms with 2024 sales above PLN 200 m, from 1 April 2026 for the rest, from 1 January 2027 for the smallest (sales up to PLN 10 thousand a month); from 1 February 2026 all businesses must be able to receive invoices in KSeF. — [ksef.podatki.gov.pl](https://ksef.podatki.gov.pl/informacje-ogolne-ksef-20/podstawy-prawne-oraz-kluczowe-terminy/); [MF stages](https://ksef.podatki.gov.pl/etapy-wdrozenia-ksef)
- Invoices to consumers (B2C) are voluntary in KSeF before and after 1 February 2026 (art. 106ga ust. 2 of the VAT Act); a B2C invoice issued outside KSeF is marked BFK in JPK_V7; until 31 December 2026 receipts with NIP and cash-register invoices are outside KSeF. — [MF: consumers](https://ksef.podatki.gov.pl/konsumenci-i-osoby-fizyczne); [Infakt](https://www.infakt.pl/blog/ksef-faktury-dla-osob-fizycznych-obowiazek-czy-dobrowolnosc/)
- VAT rates: 5% for e-books and audiobooks (not for publications dominated by video or music, which take 23%); online courses, webinars and PDF training materials are treated as 23% (accounting-blog source, no legal basis cited). — [Podatnik.info](https://www.podatnik.info/publikacje/opodatkowanie-e-bookow-i-audiobookow,659181); [Solidna Księgowa](https://solidnaksiegowa.com/jaka-stawka-vat-przy-sprzedazy-e-booka/)
- Cross-border B2C: EUR 10,000 (PLN 42,000) EU-wide threshold; above it the buyer's country VAT applies via OSS; self-paced video courses are electronically supplied services. — [iFirma OSS](https://www.ifirma.pl/blog/podatki/vat-oss/); [Freenance](https://freenance.io/praca-zdalna/vat-oss-uslug-cyfrowych-zdalnie-2026-pl-procedura-mf-rejestracja-progi-przyklad-saas-streaming/)
- Cash register: tax ruling 0114-KDIP1-3.4012.211.2022.2.KP confirmed that sales of electronic services (online courses and e-books) can be exempt from cash-register recording under § 2 of the exemption regulation; the e-book must be intangible (not on a physical medium); the PLN 20,000 limit refers to total sales to consumers. — [Poradnik Przedsiębiorcy](https://poradnikprzedsiebiorcy.pl/-sprzedaz-e-bookow-a-kasa-fiskalna)

### Inferences
- A two-to-three-month price test under the quarterly limit needs no CEIDG registration, but consumer law, GDPR and PKE apply in full to unregistered sellers.

### Gaps
- Sources conflict on the VAT subjective exemption limit (PLN 200,000 versus 240,000 for 2026) — to verify in art. 113 of the VAT Act. The current exemption regulation's number, annex position and validity period were not confirmed. Whether a live cohort course qualifies for the education VAT exemption was not researched.

## 12. EU AI Act transparency duties; copyright in AI-generated content

### Takeaway
AI Act art. 50 applies from 2 August 2026 (chatbots must disclose they are AI; synthetic content must be marked), with a deferral of the marking duty to 2 December 2026 for generative systems already on the market; purely AI-generated content is widely regarded as unprotected by copyright.

### Cited Findings
- Art. 50 applies from 2 August 2026 and was largely untouched by the Digital Omnibus, except art. 50(2) marking: deferred to 2 December 2026 for generative systems already on the market, and to 2 February 2027 for watermark-detection interoperability. — [Ropes & Gray](https://www.ropesgray.com/en/insights/viewpoints/2026/08/102nfqm/you-talkin-to-me-operationalising-the-eu-ai-acts-transparency-obligations); [CMS](https://cms.law/en/esp/legal-updates/eu-ai-act-developments-key-political-agreement-on-the-digital-omnibus-on-ai-implementation-timeline-and-transparency-consultation)
- A Code of Practice on marking and labelling AI-generated content: draft published around January 2026; one firm reports it was finalised on 10 June 2026 and assessed as adequate, another source says it is still in progress — conflicting. — [Jones Day](https://jonesday.com/en/insights/2026/01/european-commission-publishes-draft-code-of-practice-on-ai-labelling-and-transparency); [Paul Weiss](https://www.paulweiss.com/insights/client-memos/eu-finalises-transparency-rules-for-ai-generated-content); [Cookiebot](https://www.cookiebot.com/en/eu-ai-act-article-50-transparency-compliance/)
- Copyright: dominant Polish view is that output generated by AI without significant human contribution is not a "work"; human creative decisions with AI as a tool can be protected; ZAiKS (June 2024) accepts AI-assisted works and requires declaring the AI share; a European Parliament report of 10 March 2026 states a work must be created by a human. — [Prawo.pl](https://www.prawo.pl/prawo/prawo-autorskie-a-sztuczna-inteligencja,531438.html); [PARP](https://www.parp.gov.pl/component/content/article/86364:wytwory-powstale-przy-udziale-ai-w-jaki-sposob-je-chronic); [Prawo.pl on ZAiKS](https://www.prawo.pl/biznes/rejestracja-utworow-stworzonych-z-wykorzystaniem-ai-zmiany-w-regulaminie-zaiks,528569.html); [Poradnik Przedsiębiorcy](https://poradnikprzedsiebiorcy.pl/-generatywne-ai-a-prawa-autorskie)

### Inferences
- An e-book or template pack generated wholly by AI can be copied by competitors without infringing copyright; documented human authorship and editing is a commercial asset, not only a compliance matter.
- An AI sales chat on the product page must tell users they are talking to AI from 2 August 2026.

### Gaps
- Whether the Digital Omnibus on AI is published in the Official Journal and the exact final dates — to verify on EUR-Lex. One commentator claims the AI Act in practice does not require labelling ordinary AI-assisted text ([Prawo.pl](https://www.prawo.pl/prawnicy-sady/czy-nalezy-oznaczac-materialy-wytworzone-przez-ai,531626.html)); scope of the art. 50(4) text exception — to verify.

## 13. New and upcoming EU rules through 2026: Digital Fairness Act, withdrawal button, European Accessibility Act

### Takeaway
The withdrawal-button directive has applied at EU level since 19 June 2026 but Poland has not transposed it; the Digital Fairness Act was proposed on 11 November 2026 as a directly applicable regulation; micro-enterprises are exempt from the accessibility act for services.

### Cited Findings
- Directive (EU) 2023/2673: from 19 June 2026 online sellers must provide an easily accessible withdrawal function (for example a "withdraw from contract here" button), two-step: statement form plus separate confirmation.
